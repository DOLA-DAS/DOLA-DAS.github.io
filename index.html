Who I Am

About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh. I joined KUET as a Lecturer in July 2019 and was promoted to Assistant Professor in May 2022.

I completed my M.Sc. with a perfect CGPA of 4.00/4.00, with a thesis on a secure framework for multi-party skyline queries. My work lies at the intersection of cryptography, data privacy, and machine learning.

Research Interests:

Applied Cryptography Information Security Privacy Preserving Information Retrieval Skyline Queries Machine Learning
